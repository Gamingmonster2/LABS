Request a subdomain

Anyone can get a free name.labs.ly subdomain. It only takes four simple steps, and you do not need any coding experience.

  1. Fork the repository. Click the Fork button to copy the project into your own GitHub account.
  2. Create your file. Go to the cnames/ folder and create a new file named after your subdomain, for example myname.json.
  3. Add your link. Put your project link inside the file using this simple format: 
    {"target": "https://username.github.io/project"}
  4. Open a Pull Request. Send the request, and as soon as it is merged your subdomain goes live automatically!

Acceptable Use Policy

labs.ly exists to help real, honest projects. By requesting or using a subdomain you agree to this policy. Violations lead to removal of the subdomain and the associated DNS record.

1. Real, live projects only

The target must be a working website for a genuine project. Placeholder pages, empty repositories, link shorteners and parked or for-sale pages are not allowed.

2. No advertising, tracking or spam

No ad networks, pop-ups, forced redirects, affiliate spam, crypto faucets, or aggressive tracking. Content must be suitable for a general audience.

3. No abusive or illegal content

No harassment, hate speech, sexual content involving minors, malware, phishing, fraud, piracy, or anything that violates applicable law or the rights of others.

4. No impersonation or brand abuse

Do not request names that impersonate labs.ly, its maintainers, or any third-party brand. Reserved and misleading names are rejected automatically.

5. Security and integrity

Do not use a subdomain for credential harvesting, malware distribution, botnets, or to misrepresent your relationship with labs.ly. We may suspend any subdomain at any time to protect the domain's reputation.

6. Enforcement and takedown

We review reports of abuse and, where warranted, remove the entry, revoke the DNS record and block future requests. Report abuse via the repository's issue tracker.

7. No warranty

Subdomains are provided free of charge, as-is, with no guarantee of availability.
